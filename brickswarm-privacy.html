BrickSwarm privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

BrickSwarm is a brick-breaker game for iPhone, iPad and Android published by BamaApps LLC ("we"). This policy covers the current beta version.

What the game collects

Nothing. BrickSwarm has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet to play.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, unlocked items and settings) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

TestFlight and app stores

If you install the beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's own privacy policy. We see that feedback and those crash reports so we can fix problems.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version adds features that handle data, such as ads or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@bamaappsllc.com.
