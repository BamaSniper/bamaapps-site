How the AI coach works

Built in today Live

  • Reads your own training, nutrition and recovery data
  • Answers questions in the context of your goals and history
  • Weekly check-ins with insights and calorie adjustment suggestions
  • Keeps working offline with a simpler on-device fallback

Agentic coaching In development

  • Adjusts your program after a missed or tough week
  • Rebalances macros as your weight trends change
  • Builds a PT test prep block around your test date
  • Every change waits for your approval

About us

Who's behind BamaApps

BamaApps was founded by a U.S. Air Force veteran and senior systems engineer with more than a decade of building and running software for mission-critical, high-security environments.
